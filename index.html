Privacy by design

Your data stays on your device.

灵镜 collects no data on any server we operate. There is no server. The full data flow is local.

No account

No sign-up. No email.

You install the app and start using it. There is no account, no email verification, no password to forget.

No tracking

No analytics. No ads. No third-party SDKs.

灵镜 does not bundle Firebase, Google Analytics, Sentry, Adjust, or any tracking library. There are no ads in the current version.

Your LLM, your call

Your API key never leaves your device.

If you configure an LLM API key, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and sent directly to the provider you selected — not to us.

Right to be forgotten

Delete the app. That's it.

Since we have no server, there is no data to delete on our end. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
