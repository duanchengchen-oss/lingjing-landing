Privacy by design

Your data stays on your device.

灵镜 keeps no data on any server. The one optional cloud feature answers and forgets.

No account

No sign-up. No email.

You install the app and start using it. There is no account, no email verification, no password to forget.

No tracking

No analytics. No ads. No third-party SDKs.

灵镜 does not bundle Firebase, Google Analytics, Sentry, Adjust, or any tracking library. There are no ads.

Local by default

The free app has no upload path.

Casting, readings and archives are computed and stored on this phone — flip on airplane mode and they still work. The single network path in the app is the optional, member-only cloud deep reading, which exists only after you switch it on.

Right to be forgotten

Delete the app. That's it.

Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Cloud deep readings are discarded after each answer — none of your content exists server-side to delete. The relay keeps only fourteen days of count-and-latency records, with nothing readable inside.
