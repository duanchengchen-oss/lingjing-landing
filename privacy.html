English

灵镜 (Magic Mirror) knows you by birth moment, not by account. Data stays on this device — unless you turn on cloud deep readings Your birth data, saved profiles, castings and readings are stored only on this phone (Apple's on-device SwiftData store). No accounts, no sign-up. Every divination engine runs locally — casting works in airplane mode. Free features never make a network request. How readings are made By default, deep readings are generated on this device — by Apple Intelligence where supported, or a built-in deterministic template — and your birth data never leaves the phone. Members may additionally enable "cloud deep readings": only then, your chart facts and questions travel over an encrypted connection to the mirror's own relay server and on to a large language model for reasoning. Answers are returned and discarded — the relay keeps no logs of your content and knows no account; it retains only a fourteen-day record of counts and latency, which contains no content and cannot be traced back to you. Switch it off and everything returns on-device; while it is off, the app sends nothing. About iCloud Backup If you enable iCloud Backup in iOS, this phone's 灵镜 data is included in your own iCloud backup (encrypted and held by Apple). That is the iOS backup system, not the app uploading; you can exclude 灵镜 from backup in system Settings. No tracking No ads. No analytics, statistics or tracking SDKs. No device identifiers, no user profiling. Purchases Subscriptions and one-cast purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, renewal and refunds; the app never sees your payment details and keeps no purchase records on any server. Notifications and widgets Reminders (such as the evening reflection) are locally scheduled notifications, off by default and switchable at any time. If you enable the lock-screen or home-screen widgets, display data reaches them only through the on-device app group — it still never leaves this phone. Your data, your call Because everything lives on this device, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing to request from anyone. Cloud deep readings are discarded after each answer — no conversation content exists on the server to delete; the fourteen-day count-and-latency record contains no content and expires on its own. Changes If data handling ever changes, this page changes with the version, and the change is named in the release notes. Contact For questions, use the support channel listed on the App Store page. (Updated July 2026)

中文

灵镜是一面只认生辰、不认账号的镜。 【数据只在此机——除非你亲手开启云上深读】 生辰、性别、所存档案、每一次排盘与解读，只保存在这部手机上（Apple 的本地 SwiftData 存储）。灵镜不设账号，无需注册。排盘、六爻、梅花、黄历、紫微、奇门——所有推演引擎都在本机运行；飞行模式下，照样排盘。免费功能绝不联网。 【解读如何生成】 深读默认由本机的 Apple 智能（如设备支持）或内置的确定性文字模板生成，生辰不出此机。结缘会员可另行开启「云上深读」：开启后，且仅在开启后，你的命盘要点与提问会经加密通道送往灵镜自己的中转服务器，转交大模型推理，答毕即弃——对话正文不落盘、不建账号；中转仅保留十四天的计数与延迟记录，不含任何内容，也无法反查到你。关闭开关，一切回到本机，随时可关。未开启时，应用不会发出任何请求。 【关于 iCloud 备份】 若你在 iOS 系统中开启了 iCloud 备份，这部手机上的灵镜数据会随系统备份进入你自己的 iCloud（由 Apple 加密保管）。这是 iOS 的备份机制，不是灵镜在上传；不想备份，可在系统设置中将灵镜排除。 【不追踪】 灵镜不含广告，不含任何统计、分析或追踪 SDK，不收集设备标识，不做用户画像。 【购买】 结缘订阅与一事一占由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。付款、续期与退款均由 Apple 完成；灵镜看不到付款信息，也不在任何服务器上记录购买。 【通知与小组件】 夜课等提醒均为本机排定的本地通知，默认关闭，开关随时可改。若开启锁屏或桌面小组件，签文等展示数据仅通过本机的应用组共享给小组件，同样不离开这部手机。 【数据自主】 数据既然只在此机，删除应用即删除全部数据，无需向任何人申请。云上深读答毕即弃，服务器上没有对话正文可删；十四天的计数与延迟记录不含任何内容，到期即除。 【变更】 若数据处理方式有任何变化，本页将随版本更新，并在更新说明中写明。 【联系】 如有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面所列的支持渠道联系。 （更新于 2026 年 7 月）
