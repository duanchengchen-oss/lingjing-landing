Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-10

What 灵镜 does

灵镜 (Magic Mirror) is a Chinese metaphysics and divination app. It offers eight divination modules (八字, 紫微, 塔罗, 易经, 星座, 梅花, 奇门, 黄历) and an optional AI chat assistant that interprets the results in natural language.

Data we collect

灵镜 collects no data on any server we operate. The full data flow stays on your device:

  • Birth date, time, gender, location: used only locally to compute divination charts (排盘). Stored in SwiftData on your device. Never sent to our servers (we have none).
  • Saved readings and chat history: stored in SwiftData on your device. Never sent to our servers.
  • LLM API key (optional): if you provide your own MiniMax, OpenAI, Anthropic, or compatible LLM API key to enable the AI chat, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. We never see it. It is used to call the LLM provider directly from your device over HTTPS. We do not proxy, log, or store your prompts or responses.
  • Free-tier usage counter: stored in UserDefaults on your device to track how many free divinations you have used. Resettable in Settings.
  • Subscription status: stored in the iOS Keychain via StoreKit 2. We do not maintain a server-side subscription database.
  • Diagnostics: none. 灵镜 does not include any analytics SDK, crash reporter, or tracking library.

Network requests

When you use the AI chat feature, your device makes HTTPS requests directly to the LLM provider you configured (e.g. api.minimaxi.com, api.openai.com, api.anthropic.com). We do not see or log these requests. Each LLM provider has its own privacy policy; please review their terms before configuring an API key.

The LLM API key is sent in the Authorization header of these requests exactly as you provided it.

No third-party SDKs

灵镜 does not bundle Firebase, Google Analytics, Sentry, Adjust, or any other third-party tracking or analytics SDK. There are no ads in the current version.

Children

灵镜 is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. The app is for entertainment and reflection only — not for medical, financial, or legal advice.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / PIPL)

Since we collect no data on our servers, there is no data to export, delete, or modify on our end. To clear all locally-stored data, delete and reinstall the app, or use Settings → Reset to clear usage history.

Required Apple privacy reasons

灵镜 declares the following "Required Reason" API access in its PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy:

  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults — reason CA92.1 (access info from same app, per documentation). Used to persist the free usage counter and last selected tab.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryFileTimestamp — reason C617.1 (display to user). Used to show "last opened" and "cast at" times in History.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategorySystemBootTime — reason 35F9.1 (measure time elapsed). Used to time-cast module load.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryDiskSpace — reason E174.1 (perform task on user request). Used to display SwiftData store size in Settings → Storage.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if we add or change any data-handling behavior. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will be flagged in the App Store "What's New" section.
