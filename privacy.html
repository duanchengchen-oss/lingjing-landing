Privacy Policy

This page mirrors, verbatim, the legal text bundled inside the app (Settings → 隐私政策). Updated July 2026.

English

灵镜 (Magic Mirror) knows you by birth moment, not by account. Data stays on this device Your birth data, saved profiles, castings and readings are stored only on this phone (Apple's on-device SwiftData store). The app has no servers, no accounts and no sign-up. The binary contains no upload path at all — casting works in airplane mode. How readings are made Deep readings are generated on this device, by Apple Intelligence where the hardware supports it, or by a built-in deterministic text template. Both run locally; your birth data never leaves the phone. No tracking No ads. No analytics, statistics or tracking SDKs. No device identifiers, no user profiling. Purchases Subscriptions and one-cast purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, renewal and refunds; the app never sees your payment details and keeps no purchase records on any server. Notifications and widgets Reminders (such as the evening reflection) are locally scheduled notifications, off by default and switchable at any time. If you enable the lock-screen or home-screen widgets, display data reaches them only through the on-device app group — it still never leaves this phone. Your data, your call Because everything lives on this device, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing to request from anyone. Changes If data handling ever changes, this page changes with the version, and the change is named in the release notes. Contact For questions, use the support channel listed on the App Store page. (Updated July 2026)

中文

灵镜是一面只认生辰、不认账号的镜。 【数据只在此机】 生辰、性别、所存档案、每一次排盘与解读，只保存在这部手机上（Apple 的本地 SwiftData 存储）。灵镜没有服务器，不设账号，无需注册。应用本体不含任何上传通道——飞行模式下，照样排盘。 【解读如何生成】 深读由本机的 Apple 智能（如设备支持）或内置的确定性文字模板生成。两者都在这部手机上运行；生辰不出此机。 【不追踪】 灵镜不含广告，不含任何统计、分析或追踪 SDK，不收集设备标识，不做用户画像。 【购买】 结缘订阅与一事一占由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。付款、续期与退款均由 Apple 完成；灵镜看不到付款信息，也不在任何服务器上记录购买。 【通知与小组件】 夜课等提醒均为本机排定的本地通知，默认关闭，开关随时可改。若开启锁屏或桌面小组件，签文等展示数据仅通过本机的应用组共享给小组件，同样不离开这部手机。 【数据自主】 数据既然只在此机，删除应用即删除全部数据，无需向任何人申请。 【变更】 若数据处理方式有任何变化，本页将随版本更新，并在更新说明中写明。 【联系】 如有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面所列的支持渠道联系。 （更新于 2026 年 7 月）
